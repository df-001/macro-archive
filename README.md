## Usage

### Blocktales

#### Autoblock

#### Farmer
Requires the following:
 - Pit floor 50 unlocked
 - Windforce unlocked
 - Baller & Knight disabled

Recommended:
 - 1080p resolution
 - Training wheels card enabled

> [!NOTE]
> Resolutions other than 1080p are experimental and may not work as expected.


> [!IMPORTANT]
> If floor 60 is not unlocked change the value in `config.ini` before running.
